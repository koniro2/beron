Week 1 Experience

Each day started with cleaning to maintain a tidy office. I assisted with various tasks, such as organizing data in Excel, greeting visitors, and helping my classmates with their assignments. One day, Ma’am Sherlyn asked us to design a hiring poster using Canva, which I worked on to make it professional and eye-catching.

Later in the week, I was assigned to organize several files into one comprehensive document, ensuring accuracy and efficiency. On the final day, with fewer tasks, I focused on keeping the office clean and welcoming for the upcoming week. Throughout the week, I contributed to maintaining an organized and efficient workspace.

Week 2 Experience

During the second week of immersion, we started each day with cleaning to maintain the office's organization. On the first day, Ma'am Glecy instructed us to clean before starting any tasks, and later, Ma'am Alay assigned me to organize and compile applicant resumes into one file, ensuring everything was properly arranged. The next day, after our usual cleaning, Ma'am Sherlyn asked us to design a hiring poster using Canva, where I focused on creating a professional and detailed layout.

For the rest of the week, my tasks included assisting visitors at the front door, helping classmates with office duties, and organizing files to keep operations smooth. On the final day, since the others had completed their immersion, I was alone in the office and took the opportunity to do a thorough cleaning, ensuring everything was in order for the following week. Looking back, I felt accomplished with the work I had done throughout the week.
